ReproCrowd

Crowdsourcing AI reproductions

Use an AI-assisted workflow to rebuild a paper's analysis from the procedures it describes, run it on its published data, and check whether the numbers reproduce. Review the result, sign it with your name, and upload it for the entire community to find it.

What is ReproCrowd?

Many researchers already attempt to reproduce the results of papers in their field with the help of AI, but in an unsystematic way. We provide a standardized prompt, a way for you to sign and submit results, and the means to disseminate your reports in a human- and machine-friendly way.

  1. 1

    Choose an article

    Pick a paper from your field with openly available data. Copy its DOI.

  2. →
  3. 2

    Run the reproducibility check

    Go to the ReproCrowd app and plug in an LLM coding agent. Together with the help of AI, claims and corresponding findings are extracted, code to re-check them is identified or created, analyses are run, and numbers are compared.

  4. →
  5. 3

    Check & sign

    Look at every number and its provenance in the report, then sign the result with your name and ORCID.

  6. →
  7. 4

    Share & compare

    The package is deposited on Zenodo as a citable record with a DOI that links to the paper, for anyone to find, re-run or compare. Reports are comparable because the agent and model are recorded.

The ReproHack ABC

Checking a research article's numerical reproducibility is now easier than ever. We think that this is also a reason to agree on a minimum standard that such checks should adhere to.

A

Accessible for humans

While results are archived in machine-readable formats, the primary purpose is to give humans access to checks for numerical reproducibility. If done on published articles, all elements of the check should be accessible and reproducible.

B

Blunt

There are many reasons why results can be irreproducible, but finding out which apply exceeds the scope of ReproCrowd reports. Results from the reports are thus stated directly without further evaluation. Transparent publications of all reports allow meta-scientists to assess which AI models and which reasons may affect reproducibility.

C

Claim-level

A research article rarely has only a single claim but consists of multiple claims. ReproCrowd checks claims individually for reproducibility.

D

Discussion starter

The reports should be considered a starting point for scientific conversations. Refer to it on PubPeer, share your thoughts and own findings, or comment on why your own research could not be reproduced or how the report affects the original conclusions.

Download

ReproCrowd ships as a self-contained desktop app for AI-assisted computational reproductions. Download the latest release, or grab the source and build it yourself.
