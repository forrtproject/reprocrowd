ReproCrowd

Crowdsourcing AI reproductions

Use an AI-assisted workflow to rebuild a paper's analysis from the procedures it describes, run it on its published data, and check whether the numbers reproduce. Review the result, sign it with your name, and upload it for the entire community to find it.

What is ReproCrowd?

Many researchers already attempt to reproduce the results of papers in their field, but in an unsystematic way. We provide a standardized prompt, a way for you to sign and submit results, and the means to disseminate your reports in a human- and machine-friendly way.

  1. 1

    Choose an article

    Pick a paper from your field with openly available data. Copy its DOI.

  2. →
  3. 2

    Run the reproducibility check

    Go to the ReproCrowd app and complete the process.

  4. →
  5. 3

    Submit & get signed

    Your agent submits a standardized, machine- and human-readable report to a GitHub repo, where a human reviews and signs it.

  6. →
  7. 4

    Share & compare

    The report lands in FLoRA with an AI tag, and anyone can re-run your reprocheck or comment. Reports are comparable because the engine and model are recorded.

How a reproduction is made

The end-to-end flow: from a paper someone wants to check, through acquiring and parsing the full text, to tagging targets, generating code, and publishing the reproduction. Annotations and open questions are omitted here for clarity.

Download

ReproCrowd ships as a self-contained desktop app for AI-assisted computational reproductions. Download the latest release, or grab the source and build it yourself.

Download the latest release View source on GitHub

The app bundles its own Python and backend, so there is nothing else to install. On the release page, pick the installer for your platform (Windows, macOS or Linux).
