### 2. Author's choices (1) to (3)

1. **Misreading vs hallucination ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L495-L515)): ACCEPT.** Requiring a uniquely identifiable source number inside the bounded local neighbourhood (same paragraph, or same row or column of the same table) while allowing compound transcription errors ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L500-L505)) is fairer for a one-instance hard gate: a genuine multi-character OCR or transcription slip (such as a 100x percentage slip combined with a misread digit) on an existing local number is a transcription error, not an invented printed result, and still loses extraction recall and result credit.
2. **Scope including footnotes, table notes, and figure captions, with explicit exclusions and numeric-only verbal bounds ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L97-L108)): ACCEPT.** Papers regularly report sample sizes, test statistics, and p values in footnotes, table notes, and figure captions. Because non-result numbers (asterisks, nominal alpha thresholds, confidence levels, codings, indices, literature cut-offs) and numberless verbal claims are explicitly excluded and canonical locators are fixed ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L100-L120)), two extractors can match results in those zones mechanically.
3. **Severity following rule pack section 9 (`P1` = a `discrepant` primary result, [SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L239-L241)): ACCEPT.** Tying `P1` to the `primary` flag ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L134-L136)) rather than the 4.2 vetoes keeps `severity` aligned with rule pack section 9 and with the `P2` labels in Table 5.1 (such as `G-18 C2`, [SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L375)), while the 4.2 vetoes still force status to `discrepant` ([SPEC.md](model-gating/benchmark/SPEC.md#L219-L220)).
