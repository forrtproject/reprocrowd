**Paper:** Kevin Grier, Robin Grier, and Gor Mkrtchian (2023), “Campaign Contributions and Roll-Call Voting in the U.S. House of Representatives: The Case of the Sugar Industry,” [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0003055422000466).

## What was run

The translation in [code/translated_analysis.R](code/translated_analysis.R) reads the supplied three datasets in place and runs the main district and incumbent models, the two alternate-window models, and the marginal probability predictions. Robust standard errors use the R `sandwich` HC1 covariance estimator. Stata automatically handles perfect prediction in fixed-effects logit; the R translation filters outcome-invariant district groups to approximate that behavior. This is not exact: the district model retains 187 observations, while the paper reports 186. The incumbent models use 106 observations, as reported.

The translated estimates for several coefficients are close to the printed table values, but the printed p-values generally do not recur under the R HC1 Wald calculation. The district sugar coefficient is -0.000631 (paper: -0.0006); the agriculture committee coefficient is -38.72 (paper: -36.3). The incumbent sugar coefficient is -0.000392 (paper: -0.0004). For Table 3, the alternative-window sugar coefficients are negative and near the printed rounded coefficients, but p-values differ. Full per-number results are in `certificate.json`.

Figures 1–3 call the Stata-only `marhis` package. The translation uses 50 equally spaced values and averaged predictions over the estimation observations, following the paper’s description. It does not reconstruct the package’s confidence intervals or histogram exactly. The translated sugar contribution curve’s endpoints are 0.730 and 0.000675, while the text describes approximately 0.75 and 0.20. This figure-derived comparison is therefore discrepant / not fully checkable.

## Scope and limitations

The article refers to online Appendix Tables A1 and A2; those are not generated by the shipped do-file. The do-file also does not calculate the Table 2 contribution-change percentages, so those entries are marked `not_checkable`. Accordingly, this benchmark certificate does not claim exhaustive verification of every numeric statement and appendix value in the article. The claim inventory and explicit non-checkable items are recorded in `certificate.json`.

Original data and code were not copied into this bundle. The R translation points to the local package folder supplied for this check. Console logs for the failed iterations and the final successful run are in `logs/`.

**FLoRA chain vocabulary:** `outcome_computational = computational issues`; `outcome_robustness = not checked`. No robustness or scientific validity judgment was made.
